Tend a local park/trail

Invite friends for a morning walk with gloves and woven bags. Clearing away plastic before rainfall washes it into root systems protects local habitats.

Bring sorting bins to your school

Speak with teachers about placing labeled paper, compost, and recycling stations in lunch areas.

Support chemical-free growers

Choose seasonal produce from local growers who protect topsoil without synthetic pesticides and join tree-planting days.
